About Me

My trajectory of over 3 years as a freelance developer has shaped me into a professional focused on results, autonomy, and scalable architecture. Recently, I founded NiixLabs, an initiative dedicated to connecting the physical and digital worlds through embedded hardware, IoT, and robust SaaS platforms.

I master the complete backend development flow, from data modeling with Prisma to implementing high-performance APIs in Node.js (TypeScript) and Java. I have solid experience in designing communication architectures, mastering both RESTful and GraphQL standards to meet complex data integration needs.

I apply Clean Code principles and design patterns to ensure the software not only works but is sustainable in the long term. Currently, I am expanding my technical toolkit with Rust, aiming to develop low-level, ultra-high-efficiency systems to power hardware automation and cloud infrastructure.
